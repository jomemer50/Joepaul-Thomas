02 / The Analog Photographer lens

Monochrome Silver Gelatin & Geologic Light

Working exclusively in medium format and 35mm monochrome negative films. Dedicated to documenting quiet spatial monumentality, stark tectonic coastlines, brutalist monoliths, and the quiet pauses within high-density cities.

Every exposure is manually measured with handheld incident meters and hand-developed using split-grade chemistry on double-weight fibre-based baryta paper.
