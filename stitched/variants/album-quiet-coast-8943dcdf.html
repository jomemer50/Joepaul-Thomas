DARKROOM PROCESS & PRINTS

Silver Gelatin Craftsmanship

All negatives in this portfolio were captured on mechanical 35mm rangefinders using natural North Atlantic ambient lighting. Silver gelatin prints exposed under De Vere 504 enlarger. Ilford Multigrade FB Warmtone Baryta 310gsm paper processed in archival two-bath fixer and Selenium toned 1:19 for shadow depth and longevity.
